Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign in to Spotify or Apple Music?

Nope. Squidd talks to the Spotify or Music app that’s already running on your Mac. There’s no account to connect. It shows whichever app last started playing on this Mac. Music playing on another device won’t appear.

What permissions does it need?

The first time, macOS asks whether Squidd may control Spotify or Music. That’s how it reads the current song and handles play, pause and skip. You can change it any time in Squidd’s Settings under Music.

Can I put my own mascot in the pill?

Yes. Choose a GIF or image in Settings under Appearance. An animated GIF moves while your song plays and rests when it’s paused.

Can I move it without the mouse?

Hold ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ and press the arrow keys to glide the player around your screen. Add / instead to show or hide it. You can also just drag it.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac running macOS 26 or later, plus Spotify or Apple Music.
